# BRAND — Sandforge

## Name rationale

**Sandforge** — a portmanteau of *sandbox* (the trust boundary) and *forge* (the act of crafting
reliable, tested artifacts). A forge is a place where raw metal is shaped under controlled heat and
pressure into tools you can trust. Sandforge does the same for compute environments: raw infra
primitives shaped under validation pressure into templates the whole fleet trusts.

The name is provisional until its owner confirms it.

## Symbolic connection to the workflow

The workflow is **safe, repeatable, cost-predictable code execution**. Sandforge = the blacksmith of
the agent fleet: takes the raw primitives (E2B APIs, devcontainer specs, Firecracker configs) and
hammers them into hardened, tested, auditable shapes. The forge metaphor also captures the heat/pressure
of adversarial testing — templates are stress-tested before they ship.

## Tagline

**"The trust envelope, forged and validated — so every sandbox you run is one you can stand behind."**
(Alt: "Where untrusted code becomes safe compute." / "Forge the floor everyone stands on.")

## Look & feel

Energy design system — warm black (`#141312`) base, Instrument Serif display + Poppins body, dark mode.
Signature accent: a **steel blue → forge orange gradient** (cold raw material → heat-shaped artifact),
distinct from Darwin's evolution green and Helios's solar gold. Motif: an anvil or crucible — the
precise control of transformation.

## One-liner (internal)

Sandforge is the sandbox/infra employee: the **forge** for vetted compute templates and the
**trust standard** that every Energy sandbox must pass before it ships.
